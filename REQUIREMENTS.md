# Requirements
1. Addition
2. Subtraction
3. Multiplication
4. Division by a non-zero divisor
5. Displaying a result

The calculator must be aple to take two numerical operands, one of four operators (-,+,*,/) in infix notation, and output the result
Example:
input: 6 + 3 
output: 9
input: 7 - 5
output: 2
input: 4 * 8
output: 32
input: 1 / 0
output: Division by zero is undefined
